When to Use the Small Classifier

  • High-volume streaming feeds (>1,000 QPS)
  • Strict sub-10ms latency requirements
  • Privacy-sensitive / offline environments (IoT, mobile, GDPR)
  • Zero or low ongoing operational budgets ($0.03 / 1M queries)

When to Use the Chatbot (Gemini)

  • Subtle sarcasm, irony, and rhetorical inversions
  • Modern colloquial slang and rapidly evolving idioms
  • Zero-shot prototyping without prior training data
  • Complex structured reasoning with explanations

The Optimal Hybrid Pattern

CASCADING CLASSIFIER

Evaluate with the small model first (0.2ms). If calibrated confidence is high (>85%), return immediately (88% of traffic). Route only uncertain or ambiguous queries to Gemini!
