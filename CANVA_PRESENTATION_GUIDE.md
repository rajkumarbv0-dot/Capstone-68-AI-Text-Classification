# Capstone 68: Canva Presentation Design Guide & Slide Deck Template

This guide provides practical instructions for creating a professional presentation slide deck on **Canva** (Free Tier) based on the presentation slides and charts generated for Capstone Project 68.

---

## 1. Recommended Canva Setup & Dimensions
- **Format:** Presentation (16:9 widescreen, $1920 \times 1080\text{ px}$)
- **Suggested Canva Template Search Keywords:**
  - *"Tech Data Pitch Deck"*
  - *"AI & Machine Learning Presentation"*
  - *"Clean Minimalist Tech Report"*
  - *"Dark Mode Cyber Analytics"*

---

## 2. Curated Color Palette & Visual Identity

To maintain consistency with our generated charts and modern tech design standards:

| Color Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Accent** | Royal Blue | `#1D4ED8` | Small Trained Classifier cards, buttons, badges |
| **Secondary Accent**| Vibrant Purple | `#7C3AED` | Chatbot / Gemini 1.5 Flash badges, highlights |
| **Success / Positive**| Emerald Green | `#059669` | Accuracy callouts, correct predictions, low cost |
| **Warning / Sarcasm**| Amber / Gold | `#D97706` | Sarcasm callouts, latency warnings |
| **Background Dark**| Deep Slate | `#0F172A` | Presentation background (for dark mode) |
| **Surface Dark** | Slate Navy | `#1E293B` | Card containers, comparison boxes |
| **Text Primary** | Pure White / Ice | `#F8FAFC` | Primary slide headings and metrics |
| **Text Muted** | Slate Gray | `#94A3B8` | Body text, captions, footnotes |

---

## 3. Typography Recommendations (Free Canva Fonts)
- **Slide Headings:** *Outfit* (Bold, 38–44 pt) or *Inter* (ExtraBold, 40 pt)
- **Subheadings & Metric Labels:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* (SemiBold, 20–24 pt)
- **Body & Bullet Points:** *Inter* or *Roboto* (Regular, 14–16 pt)
- **Code & Latency Figures:** *Fira Code* or *Courier Prime* (Medium, 14 pt)

## 4. Slide-by-Slide Canva Layout Blueprint

### Slide 1: Title Slide (Hero Impact)
- **Background:** `#0F172A` with subtle glowing radial gradient (Blue `#1D4ED8` top left, Purple `#7C3AED` bottom right).
- **Layout:** Centered title: *"Small Classifier vs. Chatbot"* in 46 pt bold white.
- **Visuals:** Add Canva icons: `microchip` (left, blue) and `ai-brain` or `chat-sparkle` (right, purple).
- **Badge Pill:** *"Capstone 68 • AI Text Classification Mini-Project"*.

### Slide 2: The Modern NLP Crossroads
- **Layout:** Two 50/50 vertical comparison cards with rounded corners (Background: `#1E293B`).
  - Card 1 (Left): Border `#3B82F6` (Small Classifier: TF-IDF + Logistic Regression).
  - Card 2 (Right): Border `#8B5CF6` (Chatbot: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash).
- **Icons:** Add `speedometer` icon to Card 1; add `language-book` icon to Card 2.

### Slide 3: Linguistic Challenge Taxonomy
- **Layout:** 3x2 grid of 6 rounded cards highlighting the challenge categories:
  1. Direct Positive (Thumbs Up icon)
  2. Direct Negative (Thumbs Down icon)
  3. Negation & Litotes (Contrast icon)
  4. Sarcasm & Irony (Mask / Smile-Frown icon)
  5. Mixed Sentiment (Scale / Balance icon)
  6. Modern Slang (Chat bubble icon)

### Slide 4: Head-to-Head Performance (Hero Chart)
- **Layout:** Upload and embed `charts/01_metrics_comparison.png` on the left 60%.
- **Right 40%:** Stat Callout Cards:
  - Big stat: **97.0%** (Gemini Few-Shot Peak Accuracy)
  - Big stat: **0.20 ms** (Small Model Latency, 2,500x faster!)

### Slide 5: The Latency vs. Accuracy Pareto Frontier
- **Layout:** Upload and embed `charts/03_latency_vs_accuracy.png`.
- **Annotation:** Add a highlight arrow pointing to the bottom-left cluster: *"Ultra-Fast Real-Time Zone (<1ms)"*.

### Slide 6: Linguistic Error Breakdown
- **Layout:** Upload and embed `charts/05_error_taxonomy.png`.
- **Key Takeaway Box:** Call out Sarcasm (40% error in small model) vs Slang (100% solved by Gemini).

### Slide 7: Prompt Engineering Evolution
- **Layout:** Horizontal 3-step pipeline arrow:
  - Step 1: *Naive Zero-Shot* (90% Acc, 24% parse failures)
  - Step 2: *Structured Schema* (96% Acc, strict single word)
  - Step 3: *Few-Shot CoT* (97% Acc, 0% failures, JSON reasoning)
- **Key Highlight:** *"Putting reasoning before the sentiment label cut errors by 70%."*

### Slide 8: Economic & Operational Cost Modeling
- **Layout:** Upload and embed `charts/04_cost_and_efficiency.png`.
- **Bottom Banner:** Bold callout: *"12,500x Cost Differential: $0.03 vs $375 per 1M queries!"*

### Slide 9: Responsible AI & Green Computing
- **Layout:** 3 equal columns:
  - Column 1: *Data Sovereignty* (100% on-device, zero PII transmission)
  - Column 2: *Green Computing* (99.9% carbon reduction on local CPU)
  - Column 3: *Linguistic Fairness* (Zero bias against modern vernacular)

### Slide 10: The Industry Solution: Cascading Hybrid Architecture
- **Layout:** Flowchart diagram:
  - Incoming Query $\rightarrow$ Small Model (0.2ms)
  - Split: Confident ($\rightarrow$ 88% resolved free) / Ambiguous ($\rightarrow$ 12% routed to Gemini)
  - Result Box: *"95.2% System Accuracy @ 88% Cost Reduction!"*

### Slide 11: Final Summary & Takeaways
- **Layout:** 4 bullet points with bold checkmarks, key GitHub/Colab repository link, and Thank You footer.

## 5. How to Export from Canva for Submission
1. In Canva, click **Share** $\rightarrow$ **Download**.
2. Select **PDF Standard** or **PDF Print** for the slide deck document.
3. Select **MP4 Video** if recording with voiceover narration.
